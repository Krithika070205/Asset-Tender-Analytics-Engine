Indian Corporate Asset & Tender Analytics Engine

1. Project Overview & Purpose

This decision-support platform evaluates commercial real estate acquisitions across warehouses, offices, and land parcels by automating tender document analysis. It combines machine learning valuation benchmarks with Indian statutory tax rules (18% GST, regional property taxes, and 10% annual depreciation) to verify bid fairness. By comparing client quotations against standardized market rates, the engine delivers clear, data-backed recommendations to prevent overpaying and optimize capital allocation.

2. Key Benefits & Security

  • Zero-Retention Privacy: Processes sensitive files locally in browser RAM without permanent disk storage.
  • Statutory Tax & Depreciation: Integrates 18% GST, Metro (12%-15%) vs Non-Metro (5%) taxes, and 10% building depreciation.
  • Fair Market Benchmark: Compares client quotes against fixed regional rates to prevent overpaying.

3. System Architecture

  • ML Classifier Core: Powered by XGBoost Algorithm trained on 20,640 Records (92.76% Accuracy).
  • Dual Input Engine: Extracts parameters from .pdf, .xlsx files or direct manual inputs.
  • AI Strategy Advisor: Generates actionable negotiation advice and risk visualizations.
